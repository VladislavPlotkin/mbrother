--- ENGLISH VERSION ---
🚀 Excited to share my new open-source project: Mladshiy Brat (The Younger Brother)! 

A lightweight, powerful remote control system for Linux machines. Say goodbye to complex SSH tunnels and heavy setups — just pure, fast connectivity between your controller and agent.

🛠 Key Features:
• High-performance native viewer (MJPEG/H.264/H.265).
• Seamless keyboard & mouse passthrough with hotkey support.
• "Input Lock" mode for secure remote interaction.
• Integrated file manager and real-time system metrics.

Inspired by the iconic 90s tech aesthetic, it's built for speed, simplicity, and reliability.
